📋 Actor Use Cases & Module Business Goals
Module Name Primary Actor / Role Key Use Cases (User Actions) Business Outcome
System Masters System Admin • Register Vendors, Clients & Subsidiaries
• Configure Banks, SWIFT & Currencies
• Setup Products & HSN Codes		 Establishes validated master records required for all transactions.
Purchase Module Purchase Officer • Create & Approve Purchase Order (PO)
• Confirm Proforma Invoice (OC)
• Plan Shipment Delivery Schedule
• Create Commercial Purchase Invoice (PI)		 Establishes legal purchase contracts and records foreign supplier debt.
Logistics & SCM Logistics Manager • Process Logistics & Freight Bills
• Create Assignment & Clearing (AnC)
• Map Bill of Lading (BL) & Ships
• Auto-email Customs Agent (CHA)		 Bridges commercial invoices to ocean ships and notifies port clearance agents.
Customs & Clearance Customs Officer / CHA • File Bill of Entry (BOE)
• Choose Clearance (HC, In-Bond, HSS)
• Calculate BCD & IGST Duty Taxes
• Release Cargo to Warehouse Stock		 Clears government customs and updates physical inventory stock.
Government Licenses License Manager • Register DGFT Licenses & Scrips
• Offset Customs Duty on BOE
• Claim Duty Drawback Refunds		 Saves cash by using government tax exemption scrips on import duties.
Trade Finance & Treasury Finance Officer • Issue LC Guarantees & Buyer's Credit
• Execute Outward Wire Remittance (ORM)
• Match ORM ↔ BOE for RBI IDPMS
• Lock FX Exchange Hedging Rates		 Settles foreign supplier payments and satisfies RBI compliance regulations.
Sales & Export Sales Officer • Create Sales Order & Sales Invoice
• Dispatch Stock from Warehouse
• Record Inward Remittance (IRM)
• Obtain e-BRC Bank Certificate		 Sells stock to buyers, collects payment, and completes the trade cycle.
